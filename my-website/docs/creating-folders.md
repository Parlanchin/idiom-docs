# Creating your first folder

## How to
+ Open the side panel (should be open by default but if not, the toggle button is on the far left of the navigation bar)
+ Click the **new folder** button
+ Write a name for the folder and hit enter or click the **+** button
+ The new folder should show up on the side panel

## Good to know
Everything in Idiom starts with a folder. Words (and in the future, sets, texts, resources and more) are associated with a particular folder. At the moment folders are isolated from each other, meaning you can't combine items from one with those in another.
